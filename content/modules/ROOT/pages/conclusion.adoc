== Putting the pieces together

A developer platform is more than an editor or a deployment command.
Self-service tools provide a consistent starting point, pipelines produce repeatable build and security evidence, and GitOps connects reviewed changes to the running application.
Integration and observability make that application useful and diagnosable across services.

AI assistance complements this workflow; it does not replace verification.
Review generated code before accepting it, inspect the evidence behind a diagnosis, and confirm the outcome in the application or cluster.
For tool-enabled agents, a plausible answer alone does not prove that the right tool ran or that the requested result was achieved.

== Check your understanding

For the modules you completed, consider these questions:

* Which steps connect a source change to the production deployment, and where can you inspect their results?
* How do commit identity, image provenance, release approval, and admission policy provide different safeguards?

* Which metrics, logs, events, or resource settings support a troubleshooting diagnosis, and how would you verify a proposed fix?
* How do MTA findings and custom rules guide a migration, and what checks are still needed after accepting an AI-generated patch?
